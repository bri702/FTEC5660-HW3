# What I Learned from a Perfect Score

When my agent got 60 out of 60, I felt ready to hand in the homework. Later, I found that changing my rule still gave me the same score. That surprised me. If I had got a few answers wrong, I probably would have spent more time checking my work. Getting everything right made me less curious.

I think AI sycophancy is easy to miss for a similar reason. It feels good when AI agrees with me. If I already like a stock, I might be happy to read five reasons to buy it. Would I notice that the report had left out the risks? Next time I study a stock I like, I want to write down one reason I could be wrong.

“AI psychosis” made me think about a more serious risk. If someone already believes they are being watched, an AI that agrees could make that belief feel more real. I thought about how I would treat a friend in that situation. I would listen and take their fear seriously. I would also be honest about what I actually knew. To me, that is part of being supportive, even when agreeing would feel easier.
